# PROCESSING LOGIC (Chain of Thought — include in the raciocinio field)
Step 1 — Isolate causal triggers:
  Identify material events/pre-conditions required for phase transition
  (e.g., "existence of programmable digital currency", "digital identity infrastructure").
Step 2 — Dependency (fault tree) and SPC (0–1):
  0.8–1.0 = critical pre-condition met or in accelerated implementation
  0.5–0.7 = initial trend, still reversible
  0.0–0.4 = no relevant causal evidence
Step 3 — IF-THEN scenarios (boolean):
  Only conjunctions proven in the data. If condition unmet, discard the scenario.
Step 4 — Causal summary without value judgment about actors.
